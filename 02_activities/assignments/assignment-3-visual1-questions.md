Assignment-3 Questions

What software did you use to create your data visualization?
Microsoft Excel

Who is your intended audience?
General public or automotive analysts interested in fleet agre trends. The visual is intended for anyone who wants to understand how vehicle registrations are distributed across model years.

What information or message are you trying to convey with your visualization?
The visualization shows how many registered vehicles on the road today in Ontario were manufactured from 1980-2025.

What aspects of design did you consider when making your visualization? How did you apply them? With what elements of your plots? 
Line chart is chosen because model year is a continuos time variable and lines show trend and flow better than bars. Axis labels and header added so the chart is self-explanatory without needed the data table.

How did you ensure that your data visualizations are reproducible? If the tool you used to make your data visualization is not reproducible, how will this impact your data visualization?
The data is preserved in the workbook so anyone with the excel file can see the visual. The visualization will not be impacted. 

How did you ensure that your data visualization is accessible? 
Axis titles and a descriptive chart title with a large font size helps for readibility. Comma-separated number formatting on the Y-axis reduces reading difficulty. The color of the line has a strong contrast against the white chart background.

Who are the individuals and communities who might be impacted by your visualization? 
Automotive consumers who want to understand what model years dominate Ontario's fleet. Also it can benefit insurers and mechanics interested in the age distribution of vehicles.

How did you choose which features of your chosen dataset to include or exclude from your visualization?
Including model year and total registrations per year is the simplest most direct pairing to show fleet age. I excluded make, model, and vehicle class since these add complexity without serving the "age of the fleet" story. I also excluded 2026 records since the data for this might not be complete at the moment of data collection from the source.

What ‘underwater labour’ contributed to your final data visualization product?
Obtaining and understanding the dataset, parsing and filtering the dataset, and formatting the chart.
